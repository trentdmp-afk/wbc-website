## Tip

Ask the church first — the tech contact or whoever records the sermons probably has the
original audio files and photos on a computer or CD already, which beats downloading
re-compressed copies from the website. Older sermons may exist on the server too; ask
Crownweb for a full backup of the account before cancelling.
