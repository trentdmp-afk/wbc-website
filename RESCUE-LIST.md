<section class="section">
  <div class="wrap prose">
    <h2>One Message, One Ministry</h2>
    <p>At Welcome Baptist Church we have one message &mdash; the message of the Lord Jesus Christ &mdash; and one ministry &mdash; the ministry He has given us to go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature.</p>
    <p>There are at least 10,000 independent Baptist missionaries serving on mission fields worldwide, and we count it a privilege to have a part in carrying the gospel beyond our own community. By faith we give to worldwide missions, believing that God&rsquo;s work is to be supported God&rsquo;s way.</p>

    <h2>Hispanic Ministry &mdash; Collinsville, Alabama</h2>
    <p>God has blessed our church with a flourishing Hispanic ministry in the town of Collinsville, Alabama. Many souls have been saved and the gospel is being preached to the Hispanic community week by week. We are proud of our brothers in Christ and marvel at how God has blessed this work.</p>
  </div>
</section>
