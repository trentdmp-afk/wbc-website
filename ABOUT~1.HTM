About Our Church

Serving the Lord since 1968

Who We Are

Welcome Baptist Church is an independent Baptist church located in the Ball Play community, just east of Gadsden, Alabama. The church was established in 1968; the first pastor was Bro. Robert Patterson. Bro. Roger Butler has been the pastor at Welcome Baptist Church since 1988.

There are more than 15,000 independent Baptist churches in America and at least 10,000 independent Baptist missionaries serving on mission fields worldwide.

Every independent Baptist church is an autonomous congregation. We are self-governing and self-supporting. The head of the church is the Lord Jesus Christ. The Bible is our sole authority for faith and practice. It is our desire to be a truly New Testament church.

A Message from Our Pastor

We are delighted to have you visit our church website. It is my privilege to serve as the pastor of the Welcome Baptist Church. Our heart-felt prayer is for people to know the Lord Jesus Christ as their personal Savior and become actively involved in a Bible-believing, Bible-preaching church that cares for them.

We make no apology for strong preaching, sound doctrine, and devotion to the Lord Jesus Christ and His Word.

— Pastor Roger D. Butler · Matthew 6:33

Hispanic Ministry

Though we have one message — the message of the Lord Jesus Christ — and one ministry — the ministry He has given us to go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature — God has blessed our church with a growing Hispanic ministry in the town of Collinsville, Alabama. This work has flourished with many souls saved and the gospel preached to the Hispanic community. We are proud of our brothers in Christ and marvel at how God has blessed this work.
